## DockPops Companion 4.3

- Clicking a pinned Pop app no longer triggers a macOS "wants to access data from other apps" permission prompt. Pop apps now open quietly the first time, every time.
- Renaming a Pop updates its Pop app's Dock tile name in place. The icon you pinned keeps working — no more dead "?" tiles after a rename.
- Deleting a Pop automatically removes its leftover Pop app from ~/Applications/DockPops.
- Pop app icons refresh on their own when artwork changes — no click needed — and older icons that had an extra white border rebake automatically.
- The "drag to Dock" button for renamed Pops in the App Store version of DockPops now works again (paired with the DockPops 4.3 update).

### If a pinned icon ever shows a "?"
A small number of icons pinned by much older versions may show a "?" after this update. Just drag that Pop app from ~/Applications/DockPops back into the Dock once and it will work again.
