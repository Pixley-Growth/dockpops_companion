## DockPops Companion 4.0

- Drop files, folders, or apps onto a Pop's Dock icon and they are handed straight to that Pop in DockPops — the same spring-loaded drop you get on the main DockPops icon, now on every individual Pop tile.
- Fixes Menu Bar mode: clicking a Pop's Dock icon while DockPops runs from the menu bar now opens the Pop at its own Dock tile instead of wrongly anchoring the popover to the menu bar.
- Version-aligned to DockPops 4.0 for release parity with the main app.
